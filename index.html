Who We Are

More than an organization.
A brotherhood with purpose.

Brother2Brother creates space for men at Arkansas State to build meaningful relationships while developing academically, professionally, socially, and personally.

Through mentorship, campus programming, community service, wellness, networking, and leadership development, B2B helps members grow together and give back together.

Our Story

Building the Brothers of Tomorrow since 2004.

Brother II Brother was founded at Arkansas State University–Jonesboro in 2004 with the guidance of Dr. Lonnie Williams, Dr. Herman Strickland, and Tony Thomas.

From the beginning, the organization was created around academic success, personal growth, cultural awareness, accountability, mentorship, and a stronger sense of brotherhood.

Official Motto
“Building the Brothers of Tomorrow”
Creed

Building the brothers of tomorrow by educating, appreciating, and connecting them with the men of today.

Foundation

B2B’s founding mission emphasized retention and graduation, intellectual growth, social enrichment, accountability, and helping men understand the responsibilities of manhood.

Frequently Asked Questions

New to B2B?

Here are the quickest answers for getting connected with Brother2Brother at Arkansas State.

Ask Us a Question
How do I get involved with Brother2Brother?

Follow B2B on Instagram, come to an interest meeting or campus event, and submit your dues through the A-State Marketplace when you’re ready to make your membership official.

Where do I pay membership dues?

Dues are submitted through the official A-State Marketplace payment portal. You can use the QR code in the Join section or the “Pay Dues” button at the top of the website.

Where are B2B announcements posted?

Follow @brother2brother.asu for meeting announcements, event graphics, and brotherhood moments. You can also join the B2B GroupMe for direct updates.

What is the next interest meeting?

The next interest meeting is September 3 at 3:00 PM in Mockingbird on the 3rd Floor of the Reng Student Union.

Who can I contact if I have questions?

Email b2b.asu@outlook.com, or reach out to the President, Vice President, or Advisor listed in the Contact section below.

Encouraging Words

Keep going, Brother.

Midterms and finals can get heavy. Remember that academic pressure is a season, not your identity. Stay connected, ask for help, and keep moving forward.

Midterms

Stay steady.

Do what is in front of you, one assignment and one exam at a time. Use your resources early, protect your rest, and let your brothers know when you need support.

Finals Week

Finish strong.

You have already made it through more than you realize. Prepare well, give your best effort, and remember that one grade does not define the totality of who you are or what you can become.
